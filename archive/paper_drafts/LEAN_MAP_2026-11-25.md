## E. Recommended order for the remaining work

1. **Structural bound, upper half** (`dim W ≤ 2ω`): the load-bearing estimate for §VII.
2. **Monotonicity and saturation**: small, and it is what turns the threshold problem into a
   single instance in §II.
3. **Structural bound, lower half** (`ω+1 ≤ dim W`): eigenvalue argument; needs
   `IsAlgClosed` and the graph description of $W$ when $\dim W=\omega$.
4. **Minimum order** (`prop:minorder`): after 1–3.
5. Everything else is either blocked on the expert (items 3 and 5 of `91_lean_followup.md`)
   or deliberately paper-level.
